For young readers — and for gathering

Children's books & community events

The Commons was built next door to a room where children help design media. Kids' books were never going to be an afterthought here.

The reading nook

Children's titles run throughout the shop, with a dedicated reading nook — small readers pick their own books, get comfortable, and read. It was the first corner of the store the Kansas City Star photographed when the shop opened November 11, 2026.

Next door: a studio co-designed with children

The bookshop shares its building with Makefully, the educational media company owner Anna Jordan-Douglass founded — a company that works with organizations like PBS KIDS and Fred Rogers Productions, and keeps a workshop room where children test and improve the games and designs. The shop's kids' shelf, and its attitude toward learning, grew from the same work.

Events in the works

Still early days — but the plan is already taking shape, in Anna's own words:

Discussion nights
“Where interesting people in the community can lead discussions on their expertise” — with room to let people gather and talk while they do something.
Craft events
Cross-stitching, make-your-own zine workshops and similar gatherings.
Have a topic or a craft to teach?
Email hello@makefullystudios.com — the event schedule is being built now, and proposals are welcome.

Quotations: Anna Jordan-Douglass, The Kansas City Star, Nov 17, 2026.
