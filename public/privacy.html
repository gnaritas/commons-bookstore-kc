Plain words about your data

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no third-party scripts, no analytics and no trackers. Pages are static; the stylesheet is served from this site.

What the contact form collects

The form asks for your name, and optionally your phone and email, plus your message. That information is delivered to the shop by an email service (Brevo) purely so the owner can answer you. It is used to reply to your note and nothing else — no lists, no advertising.

Map embed

The visit page embeds a Google Maps frame so you can find the shop. Google may log requests for that frame under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Railway. Server logs record basic request and contact-form events to keep the site running.

Questions

Email hello@makefullystudios.com and the shop will answer.
