The core of the shop

Nonfiction, memoirs & the big questions

Walk in looking for a good memoir and you'll find the whole reason this store exists: books about how people, and the systems around them, actually work.

Sorted the way the owner reads

Interesting people — memoirs & biographies
Lives worth learning from, told from the inside. This is the first wall the shop organized, and still its center of gravity.
Society, culture & power
“Books on a range of topics from scandalous people or stories to books on racism or systemic issues” — the section next to the biographies.
Elections, politics & systems
A lot on how decisions get made and who they get made for.
The long view
United States history, psychology and travel — shelved alongside, because “there's a lot that is digging deeper into, ‘how did we get to where we are today?’ There's some good stuff in there.”

Quotations: Anna Jordan-Douglass describing her own shelves, The Kansas City Star, Nov 17, 2026.

Reading lists, made on request

“I've kind of sorted a lot of books (by): interesting people, memoirs and biographies, and then the section next to that: society, culture, and power.”

Tell us the question you're chewing on and we'll point you at the shelf — at the counter, or by email at hello@makefullystudios.com.
