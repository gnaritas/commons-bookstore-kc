What's on the shelves

A shop sorted by the questions it asks

Owner Anna Jordan-Douglass shelved the store around the way she reads: interesting people first, then the systems around them. Every section is her own curation, chosen for readers who want to dig into “how did we get to where we are today?”

The story behind the counter

“It started to drive what became this idea of I have to pivot my thinking a little bit, but also how do I use my skills to make sure people care about learning?” And then: “one day it hit me that I love books and I could just do all of that, but in the context of a bookstore.”

Anna Jordan-Douglass, founder of The Commons — to The Kansas City Star, November 17, 2026

The Commons shares its Troost Avenue building with Makefully, the educational media company Jordan-Douglass founded — co-designed with children, and at work with organizations like PBS KIDS and Fred Rogers Productions. The bookshop grew from the same instinct: education, connection and community, in one welcoming room.

Good to know before you visit

Questions, answered

When is The Commons open?

Wednesday through Saturday, 11am to 7pm. The bookstore opened November 11, 2026 at 5508 Troost Ave.

What kind of books do you carry?

Mostly nonfiction — memoirs and biographies, books on society, culture and power, elections and politics and systems — plus historical fiction, cookbooks, psychology, travel, United States history, children's books and gifts.

Where exactly is the store?

5508 Troost Ave, Kansas City, MO 64110 — on the block with Hi-Hope's Ice Cream and Blackhole Bakery.

Is there a children's section?

Yes — a children's reading nook and children's titles of all ages throughout the shop.

Do you host events?

Events are being planned: discussions led by people with community expertise, and craft events like cross-stitching or make-your-own zine workshops. Email hello@makefullystudios.com to join one or propose a topic.
