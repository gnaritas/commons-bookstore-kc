The shelves

Every section, on purpose

The Commons is mostly nonfiction — but “mostly” hides how deliberate the shelves are. The selection is curated, section by section, by the shop's owner. Here is the whole store at a glance.

Memoirs & biographies

The “interesting people” wall — lives told from the inside, sorted by what they can teach.

Society, culture & power

Books on scandalous people and stories, racism, and systemic issues — the section next to the biographies.

Elections, politics & systems

A lot on how power actually operates: elections, politics and the systems that shape them.

United States history & historical fiction

Digging deeper into how we got here — nonfiction history alongside strong historical fiction.

Psychology & the mind

Titles on how people think, feel and change.

Cookbooks & food writing

Because a good kitchen book belongs on a good shelf.

Travel

Elsewhere, for when Troost isn't enough.

Children's books

Titles for all ages throughout the shop, with a children's reading nook of their own.

Gifts

Well-chosen gifts sprinkled throughout — easy to find, hard to put back down.

Two corners deserve their own page: the nonfiction core — memoirs, society and power, politics and systems — and the children's books and the events being planned.
